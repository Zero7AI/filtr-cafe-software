# FILTR POS — Demo Video Walkthrough Script

> **Duration**: ~4-5 minutes  
> **Recording tool**: OBS / Phone screen recorder / Loom  
> **Record the actual app** (APK on a tablet, or the web demo in a browser)

---

## 🎬 INTRO (15 seconds)

**Show**: App launch screen / POS billing tab  
**Say**:
> "This is FILTR — a complete Point of Sale, Kitchen Management, and Analytics system designed for cafés and restaurants. Let me walk you through how it works."

---

## 📋 SCENE 1: Place an Order (60 seconds)

**Actions**:
1. Type a customer name — show the **auto-suggest dropdown** pulling up past customers
2. Scroll through the **category chips** (All → ICED COFFEE → FRAPPE → FRIES → SANDWICH)
3. Tap **ADD** on "Hazelnut Iced Coffee" — show quantity go to 1
4. Tap **+** to add another — quantity becomes 2
5. Tap **ADD** on "Peri Peri Fries"
6. Show the **cart bar** sliding up at bottom — "3 items | ₹287"
7. Type a note: "Extra hot, less sugar"
8. Tap **Place Order**

**Say**:
> "Creating an order is instant. Tap items from the menu grid, adjust quantities, add customer notes. The order gets a unique ID like FLT-20260907-1001, saves locally, and syncs to the cloud in the background."

---

## 🍳 SCENE 2: Kitchen Management (60 seconds)

**Actions**:
1. Switch to **Orders** tab
2. Show the **revenue banner** at top — today's total, Cash vs UPI split
3. Point out the **per-unit kitchen tracking**: "Unit 1/2: Hazelnut Iced Coffee — Mark Made"
4. Tap **Mark Made** on first unit → shows "Completed ✓"
5. Tap **Mark Made** on second unit → order status flips to "☕ Coffee Ready"
6. Show status badges row: Making → Ready, Unpaid, Preparing

**Say**:
> "Kitchen staff sees every item broken down unit by unit. As each cup is brewed, they tap Mark Made. When everything's ready, the order automatically flips to Coffee Ready. No miscounts, no missed items."

---

## 💳 SCENE 3: Payment & Receipt (45 seconds)

**Actions**:
1. Tap the **Unpaid** badge → Payment modal opens
2. Tap **💵 CASH PAYMENT** — badge turns green "Cash Paid"
3. Tap **🧾 Bill Receipt** button
4. Show the **receipt with UPI QR code** — scannable right from the screen
5. Show the **Print** and **Share** buttons

**Say**:
> "Collecting payment is one tap — Cash or UPI. The receipt generates a live UPI QR code that customers can scan directly from your screen with any UPI app. You can also print thermal receipts or share via WhatsApp."

---

## 📊 SCENE 4: Analytics Dashboard (45 seconds)

**Actions**:
1. Switch to **Dashboard** tab
2. Show the **Net Revenue hero card** — total, paid, pending, average order value
3. Switch date filters: Today → This Week → All Time
4. Scroll to **Top Selling SKUs** — ranked list with units and revenue
5. Show the **Cash vs UPI revenue** cards

**Say**:
> "The dashboard gives you instant visibility into your business. Net revenue, cash vs UPI split, top-selling items — all filterable by any date range. No spreadsheets needed."

---

## 📖 SCENE 5: Menu Management (30 seconds)

**Actions**:
1. Switch to **Item Master** tab
2. Show the menu list with prices, categories, stock toggles
3. Toggle an item **Out of Stock** — switch turns off
4. Tap **+ Add Item** → fill in "Mocha Frappe", FRAPPE, ₹139
5. Tap **Save**

**Say**:
> "Menu changes are instant. Run out of an ingredient? Toggle it off. Add seasonal specials in seconds. Everything syncs to your cloud backup automatically."

---

## 👥 SCENE 6: Customer CRM (30 seconds)

**Actions**:
1. Switch to **Customers** tab
2. Show a customer card with order count, total spent, and pending dues
3. Tap **Settle** on a customer with dues — all orders marked paid

**Say**:
> "Every customer builds a profile automatically. You see their order history, total spend, and any pending tab. One tap to settle all dues when they pay up."

---

## 📦 SCENE 7: Inventory (30 seconds)

**Actions**:
1. Switch to **Inventory** tab (dark theme)
2. Show stock cards with health indicators — 🟢 🟡 🔴
3. Show a "REQUIRED" badge on a low-stock item
4. Tap **Add Stock** → enter packs → stock updates

**Say**:
> "Inventory tracks raw materials in real-time. Green means healthy, yellow is running low, red means you need to reorder. Add stock as deliveries arrive — it calculates how many days you have left automatically."

---

## 🎤 SCENE 8: Quick mention — Voice Ordering & Thermal Printing (15 seconds)

**Say**:
> "The native Android app also includes AI voice ordering powered by Gemini — just speak the order naturally and it parses items, quantities and customer name automatically. Plus direct USB thermal printer support for receipts and kitchen tokens."

---

## 🏁 OUTRO (15 seconds)

**Say**:
> "That's FILTR — fast billing, real-time kitchen tracking, instant analytics, and full inventory management. One app, everything your café needs. Let's talk about getting this set up for you."

---

## 📝 Recording Tips

- **Web demo**: Open `index.html` in Chrome, use responsive mode (tablet size) for best look
- **Android**: Record screen on the tablet/phone with the installed APK
- **Resolution**: 1080p or higher
- **Pre-load**: Place 3-4 orders before recording so the Dashboard and Orders screens look populated
- **Background music**: Light café/lounge music works well (keep it low)
